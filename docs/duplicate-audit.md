## Method

- **Title:** NFKC-folded, lower-cased, punctuation removed, and the words `wall`, `art`, `print`, `prints` ignored. The *headline* is the title up to the first ` | ` / ` – ` / ` - ` separator, minus any `(…)` parenthetical. Identical = same normalised headline (also checked: same normalised full title).
- **Near-identical:** headlines not already grouped whose word sets overlap by at least 0.75 (Jaccard). These can be genuine variants of different artworks; they are candidates, not conclusions.
- **Same image:** identical `image_url`. **Same description:** identical text after folding case/whitespace/unicode styling.
- **Description match** is `yes` only for identical text. When it is `no`, the percentage is the word overlap of the *product-specific* text (description lines found in fewer than 30 products), since ~90% of every description is shared Etsy boilerplate.
- **Linked reviews** = rows in `customer_reviews` with this `product_id`.
